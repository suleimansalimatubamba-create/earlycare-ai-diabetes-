4. Foot Check - Prevent Ulcer & Amputation

Check feet EVERY day. Small wound not noticed early can become ulcer.

  • ✅ Look between toes - cut, redness, swelling?
  • ✅ Feel - foot hot, swollen, or numb?
  • ✅ Any wound not healing in 2-3 days?
  • ❌ Do NOT walk barefoot. Do NOT press wound.

5. Caretaker Guide

For daughters / family caring at home:

  1. Wash hands before touching wound
  2. Clean gently with clean water, do not scrub
  3. Cover with clean dressing, change daily
  4. Watch DANGER: More pain, pus, bad smell, fever, black skin, leg swelling
  5. If danger → Go to hospital / Call Nurse Online NOW

Daily care: Balanced diet, medicine as prescribed, drink water, walk a little if doctor allows.
