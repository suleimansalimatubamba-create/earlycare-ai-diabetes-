# EarlyCare AI: Multi-Portal Diabetes Screening & Caretaker Ecosystem

## 🌍 Overview
EarlyCare AI is an innovative, mobile-first healthcare web application designed to bridge the gap between early diabetes detection, home-based caretaker support, and professional nursing triage. Built specifically with resource-constrained environments in mind, it operates entirely lightweight and mobile-compatible with zero heavy server infrastructure required.

## 👥 Multi-Portal Architecture
1. **Patient Portal:** 
   - Instant FREE 6-question diabetes risk screening.
   - Blood sugar checker, interpreter, and historical log tracker.
   - Emergency triage alerts for critical glucose ranges.
   - Direct booking & consultation fee calculator (supporting NHIS subsidized rates, standard rates, and government waivers).
   - Hospital bed availability checks and home medicine delivery requests.
2. **Worker Portal (Nurses & Caretakers):**
   - Secure worker registration requiring official Nursing Council PINs or caretaker hygiene training verification.
   - Liveness Video Verification and Ghana Card validation for safety.
   - Patient review and rating tracking (with automatic worker suspension flags for low ratings).
3. **Employer / Admin Session:**
   - High-level system oversight and monetization metrics.
   - Roadmap documentation for future biometric hardware diagnostic machines.
